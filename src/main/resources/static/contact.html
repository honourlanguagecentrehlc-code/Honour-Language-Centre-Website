Frequently Asked Questions

How can I register for a course?

You can register by visiting the "Courses" page and clicking the "Apply Now" button for your desired course. Complete the online registration form and follow the payment instructions.

What payment methods do you accept?

We accept bank transfers, mobile banking (KBZ Pay, Wave Money), and online payment gateways. Specific payment instructions will be provided during registration.

What is your refund policy?

Full refunds within 7 days of purchase if no course content has been accessed. Partial refunds (50%) within 14 days of the course start date. See our Terms of Service for complete details.

Do I need any special equipment for online classes?

You'll need a stable internet connection, a computer or smartphone with a camera and microphone, and the Zoom application installed. Headphones are recommended for better audio quality.

Can I access course materials after the course ends?

Yes, enrolled students have access to course materials for 6 months after the course completion date, allowing you to review and practice at your own pace.
